## Definition

A **Validated Denominator** is a common foundational element that meets all of the following criteria simultaneously:

1. **Atomic** — Cannot be meaningfully reduced further.
2. **Kinetic** — Involves motion, change, or energy dynamics.
3. **Fractal** — Self-similar across scales (kernel → psychological → systemic).
4. When placed in relationship with other elements, it reliably **earns concrete mechanisms**.
5. Those mechanisms **solve real, recurring user gaps** for neurodivergent individuals.
6. The mechanisms deliver **uplift** and **deterministic, real-world answers**.

---

## Current Validated Denominators

### 1. Fluctuation Dynamics
**Core Expression:** Rate of change (velocity), acceleration, and duration of any measurable state.

**Why Validated:** Fundamental to detecting dangerous trajectories before they become damaging. Enables precise timing of interventions.

**Key Mechanisms Earned:**
- Trend-based early warning
- Phase transition detection
- Controlled rate limiting during ascent phases

---

### 2. Budget / Resource Accounting
**Core Expression:** Depletable, quantifiable, enforceable units of capacity that can be spent, protected, and replenished.

**Why Validated:** One of the most powerful and cross-scale concepts in the entire system. Directly maps to neurodivergent executive function realities.

**Key Mechanisms Earned:**
- Context switch budgeting
- Executive capacity protection
- Predictive recovery scheduling
- Priority arbitration under scarcity

---

### 3. Contrast Differential
**Core Expression:** The measurable difference between two states or phases (especially load vs relief).

**Why Validated:** The primary generator of insight and psychological integration. Pure stability or pure chaos rarely produces new understanding — contrast does.

**Key Mechanisms Earned:**
- Uplift generation through tension differential
- Narrative completion during descent
- Insight crystallization windows

---

### 4. Controlled Oscillation
**Core Expression:** Deliberate, bounded, periodic movement between high-tension and low-tension states.

**Why Validated:** The structural backbone of productive psychological motion. Allows the system to engineer conditions for growth without damage.

**Key Mechanisms Earned:**
- Roller Coaster cycle structure
- Safe stress induction protocols
- Protected relief and coast phases

---

### 5. Adaptation Offset
**Core Expression:** The first-class ability to apply bounded compensatory actions in response to detected states or trajectories.

**Why Validated:** Turns monitoring from passive observation into active, protective agency. The "so what" layer that makes the rest of the system useful.

**Key Mechanisms Earned:**
- Real-time load adjustment
- Context lock and sensory gating
- Recovery enforcement
- Risk score elevation to higher layers

---

## Usage Rule

All future NeuroDiOS mechanism design, kernel primitives, daemon logic, and user-facing adaptation features should be expressed as combinations, relationships, and applications of these five Validated Denominators.

Anything that cannot be grounded in these five is considered higher-order or still-fragmented until it can be.
