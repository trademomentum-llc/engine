# Requirements Specification: Solution-Generating Uplift & Compounding Improvement System

**Document ID:** NEURODIOS-SGU-REQ-001  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Date:** 2026-05-29

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the requirements for turning "uplift" into a deliberate, continuous engine that helps the user:

- Generate concrete solutions to real-world problems (trivial to macro).
- Constantly create and protect capacity to make improvements.
- Ensure those micro-improvements compound into measurable successes over time.

This capability must be tightly integrated with the NeuroBalance Engine (health governor) and the Roller Coaster Framework (deliberate tension for insight), so that psychological state management directly fuels practical agency and compounding results.

---

## 2. Functional Requirements

**FR-SGU-01:** The system shall detect and protect "high-capacity windows" (using Budget Accounting + low negative Fluctuation Dynamics) and activate active solution-generation support during those windows.

**FR-SGU-02:** During Crystallization phases (and daily protected windows), the system shall actively surface real problems the user cares about and assist in generating actionable solution fragments.

**FR-SGU-03:** The system shall maintain a living Improvement Ledger that tracks micro-improvements and surfaces compounding opportunities (building on prior contrast and state memory).

**FR-SGU-04:** All uplift activities must remain under the governance of the NeuroBalance Coordinator — no solution-generation pressure may be applied if denominators indicate risk of unhealthy depletion or masking.

**FR-SGU-05:** The system must provide deterministic mechanisms for turning internal state contrast into external problem-solving progress.

**FR-SGU-06:** Success must be measurable in real-world terms (problems with active solution fragments, compounding rate of improvements, reduction in "stuck" time on valued projects).

---

## 3. Non-Functional Requirements

- The system must never sacrifice long-term neurological health for short-term output.
- All generated suggestions must be low-friction and respect current capacity.
- The compounding process must be visible and reviewable by the user and higher Morphogenetic layers.

**End of Requirements**
